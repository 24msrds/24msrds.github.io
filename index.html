Background

Two degrees, one internship, and a habit of shipping the whole pipeline — not just the analysis.

I'm a recent M.Sc. Data Science and Analytics graduate from Jain University, Bengaluru, with hands-on experience turning messy datasets into pipelines, dashboards, and tools people actually use.

During my internship as a Data Analyst / Data Engineer at Share Market Profile, I built ETL pipelines to move and clean raw data, then designed the dashboards stakeholders actually used to track it week over week.

I gravitate toward projects that pair engineering with a clear business question: an attrition dashboard that explains why people leave, a payroll system that flags issues before they escalate, an AI assistant that cites its sources instead of guessing.
